The 3D Artist Who Models It Production Ready

Hard-surface props, vehicles and interiors, built from real reference and handed over clean: sensible topology, tidy UVs and PBR textures that drop straight into Unreal, Unity or a V-Ray still.

Usually replies within a day. Blender, 3ds Max, Maya, ZBrush and Substance in the toolbox.

Four Steps From Reference to Engine

The same pipeline on every job, from a single prop to a full room, so you always know what arrives next and what it will look like.

01

Reference & blockout

Real-world photos, dimensions and a grey-box pass you can approve before any detail goes in.

02

Hard-surface model

Clean quads, supported edges and a polygon budget that suits the platform, from VR to film.

03

UV, bake & texture

High to low bakes, then wear, grime and colour painted in Substance with real material values.

04

Engine-ready delivery

Imported, lit and checked in your engine, with collisions and LODs when the project needs them.
